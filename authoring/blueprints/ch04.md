# Blueprint: Chapter 4 — Matrices of Maps

This chapter was not planned separately. It is the second half of the original Linear
Maps blueprint (`ch02.md`, then numbered `ch03.md`), split off when linear maps were
moved ahead of linear systems: §§01–05 of the old chapter stayed behind as Chapter 2,
which uses no coordinates, and its §§06–10 became this chapter, which uses them and so
must follow Chapter 3.

The section-by-section plan for these five sections is still in `ch02.md`, under its old
numbering:

| Here | In `ch02.md` | Title |
|---|---|---|
| §01 | §06 | The Matrix of a Linear Map |
| §02 | §07 | Change of Basis and Similarity |
| §03 | §08 | Rank, Equivalence and Factorizations |
| §04 | §09 | Products and Quotient Spaces |
| §05 | §10 | Projections and the Trace of an Operator |

**What changed with the split.** The chapter may now use all of Chapter 3 — elimination,
the reduced row echelon form, elementary matrices, the Invertible Matrix Theorem and the
rank of a matrix — which the old §§06–10 already did. What moved *into* it is the pair of
worked matrix computations that used to sit in the map sections: `exm-kernel-image-matrix-map`
(now in §01), which finds a kernel and an image by row reduction.

**Still open.** `authoring/STATUS.md` records two items against these sections that the
split does not settle: §01 is long and its rank/invertibility subsection could move to §03,
and §04 is long enough to consider splitting products and cosets from the isomorphism theorems.
