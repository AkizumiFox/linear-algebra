Linear Transformations

Vector spaces are only half of the story; the other half is the maps between them that respect addition and scalar multiplication. This chapter studies these linear transformations.

Overview

In this part, we will explore:

  • Linear transformations: the definition and basic properties, examples and non-examples, and the fact that a linear transformation is determined by its values on a basis.
  • Kernel and image: the two subspaces attached to every linear transformation, with examples and their basic theorems.
  • The rank–nullity theorem: nullity and rank, and the identity nullity(f)+rank(f)=dim⁡(V) for a finite-dimensional V.
